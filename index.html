FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach us on WhatsApp any time.

Does Phyzelyne connect to my bank account?
No. You log transactions and invoices directly, so Phyzelyne never asks for your bank login or stores card numbers. Less shared, less exposed.
How secure is my financial data?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with isolated per-account storage and two-factor authentication built in — so your financials stay private and yours alone.
What currencies does Phyzelyne support?
150+ currencies. Set a primary currency for your dashboard — GHS, NGN, USD, or EUR — and log transactions in any other. Conversion rates update in real time.
What is the Phyzelyne AI finance coach?
A conversational advisor trained to read the spending patterns and cashflow trends already in your account — offering budgeting targets and flagging redundant charges.
Can I customize the interface?
Yes — Phyzelyne's interface is fully themeable. Choose curated color palettes and light or dark adaptations.
