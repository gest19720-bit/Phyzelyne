Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026 • Version 1.2

At Phyzelyne ("we," "our," or "us"), developed and operated by PetitGenie Codes & Tech, we take your financial privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access or use the Phyzelyne personal finance tracker, business invoicing tools, web application, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily enter or provide when using Phyzelyne:

  • Financial & Transaction Data: Income entries, expenses, payment dates, categories, merchants, descriptions, budgets, and invoices manually logged into the application.
  • Account & Registration Details: Email address, username, profile settings, and authentication IDs created during account setup via Clerk or Supabase.
  • Business & Client Information: Business name, client contacts, tax identifiers, and payment notes entered within the business and invoicing modules.
  • Technical & App Preferences: System settings, currency selections, theme preferences, and session tokens necessary for session continuity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To deliver, maintain, and enhance the Phyzelyne personal and business financial tracking features.
  • To generate real-time AI-powered financial insights, budget recommendations, and cash flow reports.
  • To authenticate your identity and safeguard your account against unauthorized access.
  • To process user support requests and communicate critical system updates or security alerts.
  • To allow seamless data export and backup management under your direct control.

3. Data Storage, Encryption & Security Safeguards

Phyzelyne employs a local-first security architecture to keep your sensitive financial details safe:

  • Client-Side Encryption: Financial records stored on your local browser database (IndexedDB / LocalStorage) are protected with 256-bit AES encryption.
  • Secure Authentication: User credentials are protected using industry-standard salted SHA-256 and PBKDF2 hashing algorithms.
  • Isolated Session Tokens: Authentication tokens are handled securely and kept isolated from persistent financial records.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, monetise, or trade your personal financial data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not share your private financial data except under the following limited and necessary conditions:

  • Infrastructure & Auth Providers: Trusted third-party cloud infrastructure (such as Supabase or Clerk) strictly for identity management, user authentication, and optional synced backups.
  • Anonymized AI Processing: Prompt context sent to AI models for financial advice is anonymized and stripped of personal identifying details.
  • Legal & Compliance Obligations: Disclosures enforced by valid law enforcement warrants, court orders, or statutory legal processes.

5. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Phyzelyne utilizes minimal cookies and browser storage essential for core site functionality:

  • Session management and keeping you securely logged in.
  • Storing your display preferences (e.g., dark/light theme, currency formatting).
  • Essential performance and security validation.

We do not use third-party cross-site advertising or behavioral tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

You maintain complete ownership and control over your financial records:

  • Access: You can view all recorded financial entries, invoices, and analytics directly within your dashboard at any time.
  • Exportability: You can export your financial transactions and invoice logs to CSV files whenever you choose.
  • Deletion: You can clear all locally stored browser data or request permanent deletion of your account and cloud profile.
  • Portability: Your data is yours; you can migrate or back it up freely without lock-in.

7. Children's Privacy

Phyzelyne is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. International Users & Jurisdiction

Phyzelyne is developed and operated by PetitGenie Codes & Tech in Ghana. If you access the Service from outside Ghana, your information will be processed in accordance with Ghana data protection standards and applicable global privacy principles.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, security updates, or legal changes. Material updates will be communicated by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
